CSP historical header ambiguity
-------------------------------
Fifteen source files, including src/Cspmain.c, retain the 2001 wording
"to be used only inside tau-ARGUS". The upstream sdcTools/CSP project added
an EUPL 1.1 grant to its README on 2016-06-24:
https://github.com/sdcTools/CSP/commit/c370c044a2fe1ae0130e5d19dc3f31436bafa863
https://github.com/sdcTools/CSP/blob/9305d4c7802bac60c98c2dc69653c5c99b643184/README.md

This fork relies on that upstream EUPL grant as its distribution basis.
The earlier wording is inconsistent with the unrestricted-purpose grant
in Article 2 of standard EUPL 1.1. A stale header is a plausible explanation,
but the fork maintainer has not received rights-holder confirmation
resolving the inconsistency as of 2026-11-12. Adding LICENCE does not itself
resolve the upstream authorship/permission question. The historical
headers have therefore been preserved and this ambiguity is disclosed.
Discussion: https://github.com/trygu/pytauargus/issues/26
