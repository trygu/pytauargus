Source code for pytauargus distributions
========================================
Copyright holders and component licences are listed in NOTICE.txt and
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

Python package and CLI: https://github.com/trygu/pytauargus
Native engine and C++/Python bindings: https://github.com/trygu/tauargus-engine
Native components:
  core:      https://github.com/trygu/libtauargus
  CSP:       https://github.com/trygu/CSP
  CRP:       https://github.com/trygu/CRP
  HiTaS:     https://github.com/trygu/tauhitas
  rounder:   https://github.com/trygu/taurounder
  hypercube: https://github.com/trygu/hypercube
HiGHS solver: https://github.com/ERGO-Code/HiGHS

For a released wheel, the corresponding release's release-manifest.json
records product_commit, engine_sha and highs_version:
https://github.com/trygu/pytauargus/releases

Check out product_commit in pytauargus and run:
  git submodule update --init --recursive

The engine gitlink pins tauargus-engine; its nested gitlinks pin the six
native repositories. Obtain HiGHS at highs_version. Build instructions are
in docs/development.md and .github/workflows/wheels.yml at product_commit.
For a source checkout, use its recorded gitlinks and engine/HiGHS-VERSION.
GitHub's non-recursive source archives alone are not a substitute for
obtaining the nested component source.

The maintainers provide these freely accessible repositories and retain the
corresponding source revisions for as long as the covered distributions
continue to be distributed or communicated, in accordance with EUPL
Articles 3 and 5.
